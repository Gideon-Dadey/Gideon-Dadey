# Gideon Dadey

Software engineer in Accra. I build and ship product (web, mobile, and the APIs behind them) mostly for teams that need reliable systems in the real world: payments that settle, health data that stays private, and apps people actually finish using.

Right now I work at **[PragmaHealth](https://omnih.health)** on **OmniH**, a diagnostics and care platform connecting clinicians, labs, and patients across Ghana and the diaspora.

---

## Selected work

### [OmniH Health](https://omnih.health): diagnostics & care platform
Lead engineering on a production health platform: prepaid lab orders, clinician review workflows, patient results, and cross-border care between Ghana and practitioners abroad. TypeScript throughout, with attention to access control, auditability, and payment flows that hold up outside a demo.

`TypeScript` · `Next.js` · `Node` · `AWS` · `mobile money`

### [French Master](https://thefrenchmastergh.com): online language learning
Production learning product for French courses, conversation, and exam prep. Focused on a clean student experience and content that scales beyond a marketing site.

`JavaScript` · `web` · live at thefrenchmastergh.com

### [SusuFlow](https://github.com/Gideon-Dadey/SusuFlow): digital susu operating system
Multi-tenant system for savings companies, field collectors, and customers. Deposits go through Hubtel and Paystack so funds never sit with collectors; every confirmed payment posts to a double-entry ledger with a full audit trail. Web portals plus an Expo client for the field.

`TypeScript` · `Expo` · `Node` · `ledger` · `Hubtel` · `Paystack`

### [AfriRoar](https://afri-roar.vercel.app): scholarships & careers
Platform for African students to find scholarships, internships, and jobs, track applications, and work from practical guides. Built as a full SaaS stack with auth, moderation, and subscription billing.

`React` · `TypeScript` · `Express` · `MongoDB` · `Stripe`

### [ReLoop](https://github.com/Gideon-Dadey/ReLoop-Tracking-App): circular economy tracking
Mobile + NestJS platform for reusable packaging: QR loop tracking, impact metrics, offline scan queues, and campaign evaluation aligned to SDG 12 research goals.

`React Native` · `Expo` · `NestJS` · `MongoDB`

### Other builds
- **[Twinspire](https://twinspire-app.vercel.app)**: product web app shipped on Next.js
- **[SKUL MOGULZ](https://github.com/Gideon-Dadey/SKUL-MOGULZ-PERSONAL-)**: networking for student founders (web, API, Expo)
- Smaller API and tooling work across Express, FastAPI, and Firebase-backed apps

## How I usually work

I care about systems that survive contact with users: clear domain models, boring reliability, deploy pipelines that don't need a hero every Friday, and interfaces that don't apologize for themselves. Most of my recent work sits in TypeScript (React / Next on the web, React Native on mobile, Node on the server), with Docker, Ubuntu, GitHub Actions, AWS, and GCP when it's time to run something for real.
